Local route intent understanding · 0.20.6 (spoken destinations added in 0.20.7)

Speech: Pleos SpeechToText 3.3.1, Korean HYBRID when an owner-supplied Client ID/Secret is configured; otherwise ON_DEVICE.
Meaning: multilingual E5 small, quantized ONNX, CPU on the paired notebook.
HYBRID sends microphone audio to Pleos Speech only during the user-triggered “말하기” capture. If registration or recognition fails, the app restarts capture with the local engine. The app does not save audio recordings. The recognized text crosses the existing vehicle-to-notebook connection for in-memory route intent processing.
The Pleos client secret is supplied from android/pleos-stt.local.properties at build time. Android apps cannot keep a client secret confidential once built; share configured APKs only with trusted testers and do not publish them.

This is a bounded sentence-intent classifier, not a general conversation model.
It identifies a stop/pickup request; deterministic validation resolves existing place requests.
Neither the model nor a spoken string can provide new coordinates or vehicle-control permissions.
Ambiguous places require a numbered/name follow-up. Low confidence, negation, queries,
expired requests and changed routes do not execute. Each voice interaction is button-triggered.
The remaining original destination is kept. The current router supports one pending via point.

0.20.7 spoken destinations ("강남역으로 안내해 줘", "가까운 주유소 찾아 줘", "집으로 가자"):
the car app recognizes these with fixed Korean phrase rules (VoiceDestination.kt), not the model.
The place words go to the existing TMAP place search through the notebook; coordinates always come
from TMAP results or the driver's saved home/work. One exact name match is guided directly; otherwise
the driver picks one of up to three results by number or name (or a tap while parked) within 60 s.
Sentences about family proposals, stops or pickups, negations, questions and several actions in one
sentence are left to the stop/pickup interpreter above or ignored.

No external inference API, key or per-call AI billing is used by the laptop intent model. Downloading the model requires internet; live TMAP routing retains its existing internet and account quota requirements. Pleos Speech service usage is subject to Pleos account/service terms.
Test artifacts contain generated test sentences only.

Install: scripts/windows/Setup-VoiceUnderstanding.ps1 (Python 3.11–3.13).
Models, Python environment and prototype sentence vectors stay under ignored dist/voice-runtime.
Every normal relay startup uses that runtime automatically; there is no extra network service.
The model is not bundled into a phone or car APK. A different laptop needs the installer once.

Its published process includes schema registration/review; this build does not claim Gleo approval.
